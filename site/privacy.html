Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Summary

No download data leaves your device. SwiftGet does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data, browsing history, download history, file contents, URLs or cookies. SwiftGet has no servers of its own and contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking code.

What SwiftGet stores, and where

  • Download list and settings (URLs, filenames, sizes, progress, checksums, preferences) are stored in your browser's extension storage (IndexedDB) on your device.
  • Partially downloaded files are stored in your browser's private file storage (Origin Private File System) on your device until the download is saved to the location you choose, then deleted.
  • You can delete all of this at any time by removing items in SwiftGet or uninstalling the extension.

Network requests

SwiftGet only connects to the servers hosting the files you ask it to download (for example a website or Google Drive), exactly as your browser's built-in downloader would. These requests use your browser's existing cookies for those sites so downloads that require you to be signed in keep working. SwiftGet never sends your cookies, files or history anywhere else.

Diagnostics

The optional Drive diagnostics page runs tests against a link you paste and shows the results on screen. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically. If you choose to copy the report and share it, file identifiers and tokens are redacted by default.

Permissions

Every permission SwiftGet requests, and why, is listed in PERMISSIONS.md.
