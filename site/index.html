SwiftGet

A fast, resumable download manager for Chrome.

What it does

  • Splits large files into parts and downloads them over several connections at once.
  • Verifies every finished file (size and SHA-256) before saving it.
  • Works with sites you are signed in to, including large Google Drive files you have access to.
  • Falls back to a normal single connection when a server doesn't support partial downloads.
  • Pause/resume, a download queue and browser integration are in development.

Privacy

SwiftGet has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. Your downloads and download history stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.

Source

SwiftGet is developed in the open on GitHub.

SwiftGet is a general-purpose file download manager. It does not circumvent DRM, paywalls, quotas or access controls.
